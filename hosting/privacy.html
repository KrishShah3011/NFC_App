Privacy policy

What we collect

  • Account: your phone number, or the email and name from Google or Apple sign-in.
  • Your profile: the details you choose to publish on your card. Anyone with your link can see them.
  • Cards you save: details of people you meet, with when and where you met, your notes, tags and reminders. Only you can see them.
  • Location: one reading when you save a card, only while the app is open, only if you allow it.
  • Contacts: read on your phone to find newly added contacts. Your address book is never uploaded; only contacts you choose to add are saved to your account.
  • Photos: card photos you take and your profile photo.
  • Usage and crashes: app events and crash reports, without the advertising ID.
  • Ads: non-personalised ads from Google AdMob. We never share your contacts or cards with advertisers.

Why

To run the service: share your card, store and search the cards you save, remind you to follow up, keep the app working, and show ads that fund the free plan.

Where and how long

Data is stored on Google Cloud (Firebase) in Mumbai, India (asia-south1), encrypted at rest. We keep it until you delete it or your account.

Your rights

You can access and correct your data in the app, export all saved cards as a .vcf file, and delete your account and all its data from Me → Delete account. Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 you may also contact our Grievance Officer.

When someone deletes their account, copies of their card already saved by other users are kept by those users, like a paper card, and stop updating.

Children

The app is for people aged 18 and over.
